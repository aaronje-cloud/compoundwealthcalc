SCHD vs VOO: which is better for you?

Updated October 30, 2026 · 4 min read · By Aaron Eason

SCHD focuses on steady dividend-paying companies. VOO owns the whole S&P 500. They're built for different goals.

The short answer

VOO has grown faster lately thanks to big tech. SCHD pays much more in dividends. Many people use VOO to grow money and SCHD later for income.

What is SCHD?

SCHD picks about 100 U.S. companies with at least 10 years of dividends, strong cash flow and steady dividend growth. It pays a higher yield than most stock funds.

What is VOO?

VOO follows the S&P 500, a list of about 500 of the biggest U.S. companies across every industry. It's one of the cheapest and most popular ways to own the U.S. stock market.

Risk

SCHD's typical swings are medium, and VOO's are medium. Funds that hold fewer kinds of companies tend to rise and fall more. The best fund is one you can hold through a bad year without selling.

Which one fits you?

  • Pick SCHD if you want income from dividends, or a fund with less tech.
  • Pick VOO if you want broad growth with the lowest fee.
