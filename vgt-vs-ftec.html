VGT vs FTEC: which is better for you?

Updated November 16, 2026 · 4 min read · By Aaron Eason

VGT and FTEC are two very similar U.S. technology funds, one from Vanguard and one from Fidelity.

The short answer

They're almost the same. FTEC charges a tiny bit less (0.084% vs 0.09%). Pick the one that's easiest at your broker.

What is VGT?

VGT owns only U.S. technology companies, big and small. A few giant tech companies make up a large share of it.

What is FTEC?

FTEC is Fidelity's U.S. technology fund. It follows a very similar list to VGT and charges a slightly lower fee.

Risk

VGT's typical swings are very high, and FTEC's are very high. Funds that hold fewer kinds of companies tend to rise and fall more. The best fund is one you can hold through a bad year without selling.

Which one fits you?

  • Pick VGT if you use Vanguard or like Vanguard funds.
  • Pick FTEC if you use Fidelity or want the slightly lower fee.

Almost the same list

VGT and FTEC follow nearly the same list of U.S. tech companies, both made by MSCI. They own the same big names, like Apple, Microsoft and Nvidia, in close to the same amounts. Both use rules that cap how big any one company can get in the fund, so no single stock takes over.

The real question: how much tech?

Picking between VGT and FTEC matters much less than deciding how much of your money should be in one industry. Tech funds fell about 30% in 2022 and far more in the 2000 to 2002 crash. Many people keep a tech fund as a smaller slice next to a broad fund. See VGT vs VOO.

Quick answers

Is VGT better than FTEC?

They're almost the same. FTEC charges a tiny bit less (0.084% vs 0.09%). Pick the one that's easiest at your broker.

Which has lower fees, VGT or FTEC?

VGT charges 0.09% a year and FTEC charges 0.084%. FTEC is cheaper.

Which pays more dividends, VGT or FTEC?

VGT has paid about 0.6% a year and FTEC about 0.5%. Yields change over time.

Should I own both VGT and FTEC?

No. They hold nearly the same companies, so one is enough.

Do VGT and FTEC hold the same stocks?

Nearly. They follow very similar MSCI tech lists, so their holdings and returns are almost the same.

Does VGT own Google or Amazon?

No. Alphabet, Amazon and Meta are classified outside the tech industry, so neither VGT nor FTEC owns them.
