SCHD vs SCHG: which is better for you?

Updated November 16, 2026 · 5 min read · By Aaron Eason

SCHD and SCHG are both cheap Schwab funds, but they're opposites. SCHD owns steady companies that pay big dividends. SCHG owns fast-growing companies that pay small ones.

The short answer

SCHG has grown much faster over the last 10 years. SCHD pays about nine times more in dividends and has been steadier. Which is better depends on whether you want growth now or income later.

What is SCHD?

SCHD picks about 100 U.S. companies with at least 10 years of dividends, strong cash flow and steady dividend growth. It pays a higher yield than most stock funds.

What is SCHG?

SCHG owns large U.S. companies that are growing fast. It holds more than just tech, but tech is its biggest part. Its fee is very low.

Risk

SCHD's typical swings are medium, and SCHG's are high. Funds that hold fewer kinds of companies tend to rise and fall more. The best fund is one you can hold through a bad year without selling.

Which one fits you?

  • Pick SCHD if you want income from dividends, or a calmer fund as you get closer to retirement.
  • Pick SCHG if you're years from retirement and want the most growth, and can handle bigger drops.

There's very little overlap. SCHD's rules favor companies with long dividend records and strong cash flow. Most fast-growing tech companies pay small dividends, so they don't make the cut. That's exactly what SCHG is full of.

If you want your money to pay you without selling shares, SCHD does that much better. If you're still working and adding money, dividends you can't spend yet don't help much. In a regular brokerage account, they're also taxed every year.

Growth now, dividends later

Some people use both, one after the other. They buy a growth fund like SCHG while they're working, then slowly shift into a dividend fund like SCHD in the years before they retire. That way they get faster growth early and more income later. We walk through how that works in growth now, dividends later.

If you do this in a regular account, selling SCHG to buy SCHD can trigger taxes. Doing the switch inside a 401(k), IRA or Roth IRA avoids that.

What happens in a bad year

In 2022, SCHG fell about 30%. SCHD fell only about 3%, because its steady, cash-rich companies held up while growth stocks crashed. In strong years for tech, though, SCHD can trail far behind. You're trading growth for calm.

Quick answers

Is SCHD better than SCHG?

They do different jobs. SCHG has grown faster. SCHD pays much more in dividends and has dropped less in bad years.

Which has lower fees, SCHD or SCHG?

SCHD charges 0.06% a year and SCHG charges 0.04%. SCHG is a little cheaper.

Which pays more dividends, SCHD or SCHG?

SCHD has paid about 3.7% a year and SCHG about 0.4%. Yields change over time.

Can I own both SCHD and SCHG?

Yes. They hold very different companies, so together they cover both growth and dividend stocks. Some people shift from SCHG to SCHD as they near retirement.

Which is better for retirement income?

SCHD, because it pays a much higher dividend. SCHG is built for growth, not income.
